These are mandatory legal details for German GmbH invoices and must appear on every generated invoice (typically in the footer area)
Also allow uploading a company logo for the invoice header
All company details editable in Settings

Create Sale / Invoice:

Select buyer: Create or select from saved customers. For each customer store: name, company name (optional), address, email, tax ID (optional — needed for B2B)
Add items: Select multiple equipment items from the inventory to add to this invoice. For each item, the app pre-fills brand, model, serial number, SKU, size from the equipment record. Admin sets the sale price per item.
VAT selection per invoice: Choose one of:

19% MwSt (standard)
0% MwSt with note "Differenzbesteuerung nach §25a UStG"
0% MwSt with note "Kleinunternehmer nach §19 UStG"
0% MwSt with note "Innergemeinschaftliche Lieferung nach §4 Nr. 1b UStG"
Custom VAT rate and note


Payment method selection per invoice:

Bank Transfer ("Überweisung") — default. Shows on the invoice: bank name, IBAN, BIC, account holder, and "Verwendungszweck: [invoice number]"
Cash ("Bar") — shows "Bezahlung: Bar erhalten" on the invoice. No bank details.
PayPal — shows "Bezahlung per PayPal" and PayPal email (configurable in Settings)
Credit Card ("Kreditkarte") — shows "Bezahlung per Kreditkarte"
Only show bank details when payment method is Bank Transfer


Invoice number: Auto-generated in format "Inv-KWS-2026-1001", incrementing the last 4 digits. Year updates automatically. Starting number configurable in Settings.
Additional fields: Invoice date (default today), payment terms (default "14 Tage ohne Abzug"), optional notes/comments
Preview: Show a preview of the invoice before generating
Generate PDF: Create a professional PDF invoice with:

Company logo and name at the top
Sender line in small text: "KiteWorldWide GmbH | Steindamm 97 | D-20099 Hamburg"
Buyer details block (name, address, tax ID if B2B)
Invoice number, invoice date, delivery date, payment terms on the right side
Table with line items: position number, description (brand + model + size), serial number, SKU, quantity, unit price, total
Subtotal, VAT line (with selected rate and legal note), grand total
Payment section based on selected method
Footer with all mandatory GmbH details


Download: PDF available for download

After sale is confirmed:

Update each sold equipment item's status to "Sold"
Store the sale price on the equipment record
Store the sale date and buyer reference
The item stays in the database for history but is marked as sold
Show sold items in a "Sales History" view with links to the generated invoices

Sales overview page:

List of all invoices with: invoice number, date, buyer name, number of items, total amount, payment method, PDF download link
Filter by date range, buyer
Total revenue summary
